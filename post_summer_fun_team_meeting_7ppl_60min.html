60-minute post-summer team building agenda — fun and productive, 7 people including a manager

Back from summer — team kickoff

7 people · 60 min · in-person · fun meets focus

0 / 60 min
00:00 Summer highlight reel 10 min
Fun

Two truths, one lie — summer edition

Each person shares three things they did this summer — two real, one invented. The group votes on which is the lie. Keep it fast: 60 seconds per person. Boss goes in the middle of the round, not last, so it doesn't feel like a performance for them.

10:00 Autumn direction — from the top 10 min
Boss presentWork

3 priorities, 5 minutes

Your boss names the top 3 team priorities for autumn — no more than 5 minutes. Crisp, direct. Then a quick round: each person says in one sentence which priority their work most connects to. No debate, just mapping.

20:00 Team superpower map 15 min
FunWork

Name someone else's superpower

Each person names one teammate's biggest professional strength — something specific they've seen in action. Not "she's great" but "she turns chaos into a clear brief in 20 minutes." Go around once. The person named just listens — no deflecting, no "oh it's nothing."

35:00 Pick one fun challenge for autumn 10 min
FunWork

The team picks one thing to do differently

Choose one micro-challenge the team commits to this autumn. Vote by show of hands. It should be slightly uncomfortable and completely achievable. Pick from below — or take a suggestion from the room.

No-meeting Fridays
Block every Friday afternoon for focused work, no exceptions for 6 weeks
Weekly win post
One person posts one team win to a shared channel every Monday morning
Rotating presenter
Each week one person shares a 5-min topic — anything, work-related or not
Async first
Before scheduling any meeting, the requester must send a written summary first
45:00 Close — one word for the season 15 min
FunWork

Word + action items + out on time

Each person gives one word for how they want autumn to feel for this team. Collect them on a whiteboard — it becomes an informal team intention. Then you read out any action items from the session (challenge picked, priorities noted). Done in 15 minutes, out on time.
