60-minute post-summer team meeting agenda

Back from summer — team kickoff

· 60 min · in-person · fun meets focus

0 / 60 min
00:00 Summer highlight reel 10 min
Fun

Two truths, one lie — summer edition

Each person shares three things they did this summer — two real, one invented. The group votes on which is the lie.

10:00 Autumn direction — from the top 10 min
Manager presentWork

3 priorities, 5 minutes

Manager names the top 3 to 5 team priorities for autumn — no more than 5 - 8 minutes. Crisp, direct. Then a quick round: each person says in one sentence which priority their work most connects to.

20:00 Team superpower map 15 min
FunWork

Name someone else's superpower

Each person names one teammate's biggest professional strength — something specific they've seen in action. Not "she's great" but "she turns chaos into a clear brief in 20 minutes."
